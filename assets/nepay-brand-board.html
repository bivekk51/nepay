nepay • brand identity system

Logo Concept Board

A developer-first payment SDK identity unifying Nepal's digital payment gateways (eSewa + Khalti + Fonepay) behind one consistent, typesafe TypeScript surface.

01 • Name & Concept Anatomy

nepay = Nepal + Pay

Nepal: The market the SDK is purpose-built for, respecting localized gateway quirks, currency formats, and regulatory requirements.
Pay: Digital transactions, integer paisa arithmetic, and mission-critical server-to-server payment infrastructure.

• Multiple Gateways, One Interface

The letter N is formed by two independent geometric strokes (representing eSewa and Khalti) connecting through a central negative-space diagonal bridge.
